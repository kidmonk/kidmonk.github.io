Delete your Kid Monk account

App name on Google Play: Kid Monk. You can request deletion here without installing or opening the app.

What is deleted

When we process your request, we delete the parent account and all associated child data from our active databases, including profile information, learning progress, and related records.

What may be retained briefly

Encrypted automated database backups are automatically purged on a standard 7-to-30 day cycle. We do not keep your account for marketing.

How long it takes

In-app deletion through Settings > Account > Delete Account is immediate. Web requests sent from this page are processed as soon as we verify the parent email, usually within 7 days.

Request deletion by email

Send a message from the parent email used to create the account. Include the words delete Kid Monk account.

Email deletion request

Or write to kidmonk@geekypi.co.in.

Request deletion with this form

If you still have the app installed, you can also delete immediately in the app: Settings > Account > Delete Account.
